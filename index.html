What is TechFest 2.0?

Where FUTB
Builders Compete

FUTB TechFest 2.0 is the School of Computing's premier annual innovation festival — a three-day showcase of technical excellence, creative problem-solving, and intellectual ambition. Organised by the SOC-SRC Executive Council, TechFest 2.0 brings together Nigeria's brightest computing students to compete, learn, and celebrate the future of technology.

From a 24-hour hackathon to a structured master debate, from AI prompting competitions to high-level panel discussions — every moment of TechFest 2.0 is designed to push boundaries and celebrate excellence.
